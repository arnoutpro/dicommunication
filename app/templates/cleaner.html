Black bar

Region in pixels, from the top-left corner.

One rectangle is blacked out on every retrieved image. The default covers a 100px band across the top, where modalities usually burn in text. Non-image objects (SR, PR…) are skipped and reported as failed, not sent.

Text label (optional)

X and Y are the top-left corner of the text, in pixels. Text that runs past the edge is cut off. Gray images get the colour's brightness. Because the text is burned in, Burned-In Annotation (0028,0301) is set to YES when you add some.
