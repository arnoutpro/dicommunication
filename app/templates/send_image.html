How to find the study
An order that's already closed, with no image yet stored, won't show up in a PACS study query (Study Root C-FIND only finds studies that already have at least one stored instance) or in Worklist. Look up the patient instead — many PACS keep a patient record independently of any study — or, if even that isn't registered, enter the details by hand. Either way, Accession Number and Study Instance UID are set in step 3.
Study Root C-FIND against studies already stored in the PACS. Blank fields match anything, but a narrower query (e.g. Study Date) avoids a slow archive-wide search.
